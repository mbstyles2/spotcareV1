Our Services

Delivered as one-off engagements or recurring contracts — daily, weekly, bi-weekly or monthly.

Whether you need a home reset once a month, a running office kept presentable every morning, or a specialist job nobody else wants to touch, the same standards apply. Below is what we cover, and what each service actually involves.

Deep Cleaning

At Spotless Care Cleaning Services, we provide professional deep cleaning solutions designed to remove accumulated dirt, stubborn stains, grease and hidden grime from residential spaces. Our services go beyond everyday cleaning, focusing on the areas that are often overlooked to restore cleanliness, improve hygiene and make your space more enjoyable.

Q&A

1. What is the difference between deep cleaning and regular cleaning?

Regular cleaning focuses on maintaining everyday cleanliness, such as sweeping, mopping, dusting and general tidying. Deep cleaning goes further by addressing accumulated dirt, stubborn stains, grease and neglected areas that are not usually covered during routine cleaning.

2. Do you offer deep cleaning for newly rented or newly purchased homes?
Absolutely. We offer move-in deep cleaning for clients preparing to occupy a new home. This helps address accumulated dust, dirt, stains and construction-related residue that may remain even in properties that appear clean.

3. Do you work alongside artisans during post-construction cleaning?
To maintain quality and ensure an organised cleaning process, Spotless Care does not carry out cleaning projects alongside artisans who are still working on the property. We prefer to begin once construction and installation activities have been completed.

Residential Cleaning

Homes get lived in, and the mess is never quite the same twice. We handle routine home cleaning, deep cleaning, post-party clean-up and move-in/move-out cleaning, carried out to a standard checklist and finished to a client-ready standard.

Commercial & Office Cleaning

Your office is the first thing a client judges you on. We cover workspaces, reception areas, restrooms, pantries, glass and shared facilities — scheduled around your operations so the work happens without an audience.

Facility & Janitorial Services

Buildings with constant footfall need attention that never lapses. We provide ongoing maintenance cleaning for high-traffic environments, with supervisor oversight and a consistent daily standard rather than a good first week and a slow decline.

Post-Construction Cleaning

Construction dust gets into everything and ordinary cleaning will not shift it. We handle dust extraction, debris removal and final detailing after renovations or construction, including glass, fittings and floor finishing, so the space is genuinely ready for handover.

Upholstery, Carpet & Mattress Care

Upholstery cleaning involves the professional cleaning of fabric-covered furniture, including sofas, chairs, mattresses and other upholstered items. The process helps remove embedded dirt, dust, stains, unpleasant odours and accumulated contaminants that regular household cleaning may not effectively eliminate.

Q&A:

1. What types of furniture do you clean?

We clean a variety of upholstered furniture, including:

  • Sofas and couches.
  • Dining chairs.
  • Office chairs.
  • Mattresses.
  • Fabric-covered headboards.
  • Rugs.

2. How often should I have my upholstery professionally cleaned?
We recommend professional upholstery cleaning every 6–12 months, depending on usage, household activity, pets and general exposure to dirt. Homes with children, pets or frequent furniture use may require more frequent cleaning.

Home Maintenance Cleaning;

Home maintenance cleaning involves the regular, detailed cleaning and upkeep of your home to maintain cleanliness, hygiene and a well-organised living environment. It covers essential areas such as floors, kitchens, bathrooms, windows, furniture, surfaces and other frequently used spaces. The service helps prevent the gradual build-up of dirt, dust and grime, keeping your home in good condition and comfortable for everyday living.

Swimming Pool Cleaning & Maintenance

A pool left alone turns green faster than most owners expect. We handle skimming, brushing, vacuuming, filtration servicing and water balancing, on a maintenance schedule or as a one-off recovery. See the full pool service.

Fumigation & Pest Control

Fumigation is a professional pest control treatment designed to eliminate and control unwanted pests such as cockroaches, bed bugs, scorpions, wall geckos and other household pests. The process involves inspecting affected areas, identifying pest concerns and applying appropriate treatment methods and professional-grade products to target pests, including their hiding and breeding areas.
At Spotless Care, we work with professional pest control personnels and use concentrated and effective treatment solutions across all fumigation projects to help protect your home or business space from pest infestations. Our approach is tailored to the specific pest concerns of each property, with safety instructions and post-treatment guidance provided to clients. See the full pest control service.

How we work with you

One-Off Service — deep cleans, move-in/move-out, post-construction and green-to-clean pool recovery.
Recurring Contract — routine cleaning and pool maintenance, daily to monthly.
On-Call Support — fast-response cleaning for urgent situations, subject to availability.

Pricing is issued on request once scope is confirmed or a site inspection is complete.
