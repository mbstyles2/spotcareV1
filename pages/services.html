Our Services

Delivered as one-off engagements or recurring contracts — daily, weekly, bi-weekly or monthly.

Whether you need a home reset once a month, a running office kept presentable every morning, or a specialist job nobody else wants to touch, the same standards apply. Below is what we cover, and what each service actually involves.

Residential Cleaning

Homes get lived in, and the mess is never quite the same twice. We handle routine home cleaning, deep cleaning, post-party clean-up and move-in/move-out cleaning, carried out to a standard checklist and finished to a client-ready standard.

Commercial & Office Cleaning

Your office is the first thing a client judges you on. We cover workspaces, reception areas, restrooms, pantries, glass and shared facilities — scheduled around your operations so the work happens without an audience.

Facility & Janitorial Services

Buildings with constant footfall need attention that never lapses. We provide ongoing maintenance cleaning for high-traffic environments, with supervisor oversight and a consistent daily standard rather than a good first week and a slow decline.

Post-Construction Cleaning

Construction dust gets into everything and ordinary cleaning will not shift it. We handle dust extraction, debris removal and final detailing after renovations or construction, including glass, fittings and floor finishing, so the space is genuinely ready for handover.

Upholstery, Carpet & Mattress Care

Soft furnishings hold dirt and odour long after hard surfaces look fine. Sofas, chairs and fabric surfaces are treated with fabric-safe solutions; carpets and rugs are shampooed or extracted depending on material with odour control; mattresses are deep-extracted, stain-treated and deodorised.

Swimming Pool Cleaning & Maintenance

A pool left alone turns green faster than most owners expect. We handle skimming, brushing, vacuuming, filtration servicing and water balancing, on a maintenance schedule or as a one-off recovery. See the full pool service.

Fumigation & Pest Control

Pests undo good cleaning, so we treat them as part of the same job. Inspection-led treatment for insects, rodents, termites and bed bugs, delivered through vetted, licensed partners with written treatment plans and safe re-entry times. See the full pest control service.

How we work with you

Not every client needs the same commitment. We work under three arrangements, and you can move between them as your needs change.

One-Off Service — deep cleans, move-in/move-out, post-construction and green-to-clean pool recovery.
Recurring Contract — routine cleaning and pool maintenance, daily to monthly.
On-Call Support — fast-response cleaning for urgent situations, subject to availability.

Pricing is issued on request once scope is confirmed or a site inspection is complete.
