Swimming Pool Cleaning & Maintenance

Clear, balanced, swim-ready water — maintained on a schedule, not as an emergency.

Pool water changes daily. Heat, rain, leaves and swimmers all shift the chemistry, and a pool that looked fine on Friday can be cloudy by Monday. Regular servicing keeps that from ever becoming a problem — and protects the pump and filter, which cost far more to replace than to maintain.

What every service includes

Every visit follows the same sequence, so nothing is skipped on a busy day. You get the full list below whether you are on a weekly contract or booking a single service.

  • Surface skimming and debris removal
  • Wall, step and waterline brushing
  • Floor vacuuming, manual or robotic
  • Skimmer basket and pump strainer cleaning
  • Filter backwash and inspection
  • Water testing for pH, chlorine and alkalinity
  • Chemical dosing and balancing
  • Algae prevention and treatment
  • Equipment condition checks with a written report

Service options

How often you need us depends on how hard the pool works. A family pool in constant use needs more attention than one that sits quiet most of the week, and a neglected pool needs rescuing before any schedule makes sense.

Weekly or Bi-Weekly Maintenance
Ongoing contract cover for pools in regular use
Monthly Deep Service
Full system attention for lightly used pools
Green-to-Clean Recovery
One-off rescue of water that has been left too long
Pre-Event Preparation
Swim-ready before guests arrive

Who we service

Ideal for private homes and estates, hotels and short-lets, gyms, clubs and recreation facilities. For commercial pools where guests are swimming daily, consistency matters more than anything — a single bad week is the one your guests will remember.

Site inspections confirm pool size, equipment type and current water condition before a schedule is agreed, so the quote reflects the pool you actually have.
